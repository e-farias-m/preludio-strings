# Competitors

Landscape review for **Preludio Strings** — a free, offline-first beginner band
app. Research current as of **October 2026**; prices and features change, so
re-check the linked sources before relying on them.

## Preludio Strings (us)

- Free, no account, no telemetry. Installable offline PWA (service-worker cache).
- Per-instrument curriculum that starts on each instrument's first note and
  teaches note-first (fingering + staff + sound), including written-Bb low brass.
- On-device microphone pitch feedback (listen & match) and a tuner — nothing is
  uploaded.
- Practice songs derived from each instrument's own scale degrees, quizzes /
  reviews, practice plan & sprint, and a weekly digest for parents/teachers.
- MusicXML import/export.

## Direct competitor — Hal Leonard method Interactive (HL method companion, Hal Leonard)

Cloud-based companion to the *Hal Leonard method* method book.

- **Music Studio:** professional accompaniments, recording, tempo control,
  built-in tuner.
- **SoundCheck™** (powered by MatchMySound™): scores every method-book exercise
  on **pitch, rhythm, and intonation** as the student plays, with audio/video
  assignments.
- **Teacher tools:** assignments, gradebook, 400+ instructional videos,
  customizable resources — included free with every method book.
- Digital method books (Book 1 for band and strings).

**Pricing:** the publisher companion itself is bundled free with a method book;
SoundCheck/MatchMySound is a **student subscription ($4.99/month)**, with teacher
access free alongside active student subscriptions.

Sources: <https://matchmysound.kb.help/matchmysound-subscription-pricing>

## School platform — SmartMusic / MakeMusic Cloud (MakeMusic)

- Interactive sheet music with professional accompaniment and **immediate,
  color-coded assessment** of pitch and rhythm; tuner, metronome, looping.
- Large repertoire: 150+ method books, 5,400+ ensemble titles, thousands of
  solos; sight-reading builder; Compose notation; gradebook; LMS integration
  (Canvas, Google Classroom); FERPA compliance.
- **Pricing (per user, annual):** Teacher **$39.99**; Performer **$29.99**
  (**$13.99** in bulk of 20+); Print add-on **$15.99**.
- Mobile is a reduced experience (no Compose, Sight Reading Studio, or Classroom
  Tools on phones).

Sources: <https://www.softwaresuggest.com/smartmusic>,
<https://excelciamusic.com/smartmusic>, <https://www.k12compare.com/product/smartmusic>

## Adjacent apps

**Practice trackers / journals** — metronome, tuner, streaks, session logs; most
monetize by subscription:

- **Practis** — free up to 5 active pieces; Pro $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr; teacher
  tiers $9.99–$39.99/mo.
- **Andante**, **BANDY**, **Practimo**, **nTune** (Pro $9.99), **Tunable** —
  timers, goals, analytics, some one-time unlocks instead of subscriptions.

**Tool / fingering-chart apps:**

- **Band Toolbox** ($2.99 one-time) — metronome, transposed tuner, interactive
  fingering chart that turns green when the correct note is played, terms
  dictionary, transposition toolbox.
- **Bandmate Chromatic Tuner**, **MuseTuner** — chromatic tuning.

Sources: <https://www.thegeeksclub.com/platforms/practis-music-practice-software/pricing>,
<https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/band-toolbox/id1415584000>,
<https://practimo.app/>, <https://www.tunableapp.com/>

## Positioning

| Axis | HL method companion / SmartMusic | Preludio Strings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Book/account + $4.99–$39.99 | Free, no account |
| Offline | Cloud-first | Offline PWA |
| Pitch feedback | Cloud (SoundCheck / MakeMusic) | On-device, nothing uploaded |
| Curriculum shape | Full method book, instrument-agnostic sequence | First-note-first per instrument (Bb low brass aware) |
| Accounts / privacy | Accounts + COPPA/FERPA | No accounts, no telemetry |
| Teacher workflow | Assignments + gradebook | Lightweight weekly digest |

**Our wedge:** the zero-friction, private, offline first year of band — the
moment before a family buys into a method book or school platform.

## Gaps worth watching (not commitments)

1. **Rhythm / intonation scoring** — competitors score rhythm and intonation;
   ours is pitch-only.
2. **Sight-reading** — SmartMusic's Sight Reading Builder is unmatched here.
3. **Teacher assignments & gradebook** — owned by HL method companion/SmartMusic; our digest is
   a much lighter artifact.
4. **Recorded accompaniments** — HL method companion's Music Studio strength; we derive simple
   chords from scale degrees.
